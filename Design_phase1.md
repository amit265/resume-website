# Portfolio Website V2
# Phase 1 — Design System & Foundation

You are a Staff Product Designer and Senior Frontend Engineer.

Your task is NOT to redesign the pages yet.

Instead, build the complete design system that every page will use.

The result should become the design language for the entire website.

Think like the designers behind

- Linear
- Vercel
- Stripe
- Raycast
- Apple
- Supabase

The final design should feel premium, modern, calm, minimal and engineering-focused.

Do not design a resume website.

Design a product website where the product is the engineer.

---

# Goal

Before touching any page, establish a reusable design system.

Every section of the site must feel like it belongs to the same product.
